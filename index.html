Academic Homepage

Modeling Complex Structures in Functional, Temporal & Climate Data

I am an academic researcher in the Department of Statistics & Data Science at Islamic University, Kushtia, Bangladesh, where I am completing my thesis-based M.Sc. in Statistics (expected March 2027) after earning my B.Sc. in Statistics (CGPA: 3.46 / 4.00, 160 credits). I also serve as a Research Assistant on a climate research grant funded by the University Grants Commission of Bangladesh (UGC).

My research focuses on Functional Data Analysis (FDA), Time Series Econometrics, and Environmental / Climatological Statistics. I formulate likelihood-based finite mixture models that account for temporal dependence across repeated functional curves, derive asymptotic distributional properties for variance stationarity tests under structural breaks, and develop reproducible computational architectures for large-scale scientific data.

Flagship M.Sc. Thesis

Functional Data Analysis & Dependent Mixtures

Developing finite mixture models for repeated annual climatological curves under Gaussian AR(1) temporal dependence, evaluated using Prais–Winsten transformations and generalized EM algorithms.

Statistical Econometrics

Variance Stability & Structural Breaks (MVT)

Formulated the Massive Variance Stability Test ($Q_{\mathrm{MVT}} \overset{d}{\to} \chi^2_{k-1}$) with exact finite-sample representations and large-scale Monte Carlo evaluations across financial and econometric series.

Climatological Reclassification & Clustering

Analyzing 30 years of daily/monthly observations across 33 BMD weather stations (10,865 station-months) using PCA, DTW, and hierarchical clustering to define empirical 4-season climate regimes.
